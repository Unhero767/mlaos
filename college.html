The Multimodal Linguistic and Ontological Synthesis (MLAOS) was engineered to solve this fragility. 1. The Vantablack Root (Null-State Substrate)

A foundational data structure engineered as an absolute exception sink. Instead of allowing errors to bubble up and crash the application, unhandled exceptions are routed into this offline, zero-latency grounding layer, ensuring system uptime remains uncompromised even during extreme logical strain.
